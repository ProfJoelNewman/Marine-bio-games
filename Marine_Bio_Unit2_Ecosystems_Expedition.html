Estuarine & Coastal Reef Channel 		Crustacean enters through the gills, attaches to blood vessels at the base of the fish's tongue, and drains host blood/mucus over months, lowering host survival fitness.
4. Five Herbivorous Surgeonfish & Parrotfish Species
Indo-Pacific Coral Reef Crest & Slope 		Each species grazes a different algal group (filamentous turf, calcified Halimeda, or endolithic algae) at a different depth zone, reducing -/- competition so all coexist.

Temperate Rocky Intertidal Shore 		Sea star preys on competitively dominant mussels that would otherwise monopolize 100% of rock space, keeping habitat open for 15+ species of barnacles, chitons, and algae.
